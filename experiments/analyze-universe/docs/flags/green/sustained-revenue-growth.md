# Sustained revenue growth

## What it means

Revenue growth has persisted through four consecutive quarterly comparisons with the same fiscal quarter a year earlier, reducing reliance on one recent result.

## Calculation

For each of the latest four quarters, calculate `(current revenue / same-quarter prior-year revenue − 1) × 100`. The rule is met only when every growth rate is at least **8%**. Eight comparable revenue quarters are required, and revenue must be positive in all eight.

Growth is recomputed from reported revenue, rather than inferred from saved growth tags or the Revenue Growth Consistency label. See the [shared requirements](README.md#shared-requirements).

## Limitations

The 8% cutoff is a screening heuristic, not a universal standard. Acquisitions, currency changes, inflation and weak comparison periods can contribute. The rule does not establish organic growth, profitability or attractive valuation. It overlaps with [Profitable growth](profitable-growth.md).
