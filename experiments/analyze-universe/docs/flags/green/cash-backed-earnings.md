# Cash-backed earnings

## What it means

Positive reported earnings are accompanied by operating cash generation and cash remaining after capital expenditure across two separate years.

## Calculation

Split the latest eight comparable quarters into two non-overlapping four-quarter periods. In **each** period require:

- Sum of net income > 0.
- Sum of operating cash flow ≥ sum of net income.
- Free cash flow = sum of operating cash flow − sum of absolute quarterly capex > 0.

Equality qualifies for cash conversion. Zero net income or free cash flow does not qualify. Individual negative cash-flow quarters can qualify when both full-year windows meet every condition. Capex is normalized to a cash outflow regardless of source sign. See the [shared requirements](README.md#shared-requirements).

## Limitations

Working-capital movements, stock compensation and other non-cash adjustments can support cash conversion. Capex definitions and investment needs matter; acquisition spending is not necessarily included. Passing does not establish earnings quality conclusively, cash available per share, fair valuation or future cash generation.
