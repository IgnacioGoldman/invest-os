# Profitable growth

## What it means

Persistent revenue growth is accompanied by positive operating profit throughout the latest four quarters.

## Calculation

[Sustained revenue growth](sustained-revenue-growth.md) must be met, and reported operating income must be **strictly positive in each of the latest four quarters**. Break-even does not qualify. Required operating income and revenue must share a currency, positive revenue is required, and operating margins outside ±100% are rejected under the existing operating-margin policy.

Eight revenue quarters and the latest four operating-income quarters are required. Earlier operating income is not required. See the [shared requirements](README.md#shared-requirements).

## Limitations

A small positive operating profit qualifies; this does not establish strong margins, cash generation, returns on capital or attractive valuation. Growth can reflect acquisitions or currency effects. Passing also meets Sustained revenue growth, so these are overlapping rules and count as two met rules, not two independent strengths.
