# Full-universe Analyze — 200 stocks

Analyzed here in the Codex conversation on 25 October 2026. All saved market quotes are dated 23 October 2026; each company’s financial date appears in its evidence link.

The input is the exact existing static Analyze dataset and formatter, with the unchanged investment-analysis instructions. Companies were reviewed in sections and then compared qualitatively across the whole universe. Business quality and valuation receive equal importance; no numerical investment score was introduced. The order is an explicit analytical judgment, not an output from a replacement deterministic formula. Individual assessments were completed before assigning the global order.

**Evidence limits:** These are provisional snapshot-based judgments. Static Analyze supplies one fundamentals quarter, 12 revenue quarters and two annual rows. Dated forward EPS is unavailable; no forward forecast was invented. Conflicting periods, share units, non-operating earnings and financial-sector comparability are reflected in the rationales or Unknown tags. Unknown is uncertainty, not an automatic poor-quality or cheap classification. Technical support context is retained in every evidence record; it does not establish intrinsic value. Primary-source checks cover six selected companies, not the whole universe; quotes, source inputs and sustainable earnings have not all been independently verified. New findings are recorded separately without altering supplied metrics. See [research notes](research-notes.json).

No deterministic selection was applied before analysis and filter membership was not used to assign ranks. The independent control is retained for a later comparison. This is selection research, not realized-return validation.
