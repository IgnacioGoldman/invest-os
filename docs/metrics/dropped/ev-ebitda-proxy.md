# EV/EBITDA proxy — retired from screening

Definition: (market cap + debt − cash) / (operating income + depreciation/amortization).

Dropped because: Approximate EBITDA, incomplete debt, cash definitions, leases and mixed fiscal periods are not comparable; especially unsuitable for financial businesses.

Typical misleading situations: Approximate EBITDA, incomplete debt, cash definitions, leases and mixed fiscal periods are not comparable; especially unsuitable for financial businesses.

Reconsider only with: Reconciled EBITDA, complete enterprise-value inputs and transparent standardized financial definitions.

Excluded from default cards, saved-filter matching, rankings, composite scores and automatic positive/negative ratings. Raw source facts and historical calculations remain available to AI research.
