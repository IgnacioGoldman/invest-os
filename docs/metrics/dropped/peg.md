# PEG — retired from screening

Definition: Trailing P/E divided by historical three-year EPS CAGR expressed as percentage points.

Dropped because: Distorted GAAP earnings affect both the P/E numerator and historical EPS growth denominator. Historical growth is not a forecast.

Typical misleading situations: Distorted GAAP earnings affect both the P/E numerator and historical EPS growth denominator. Historical growth is not a forecast.

Reconsider only with: A sourced, dated forward growth horizon, reconciled earnings and a methodology appropriate for the business.

Excluded from default cards, saved-filter matching, rankings, composite scores and automatic positive/negative ratings. Raw source facts and historical calculations remain available to AI research.
