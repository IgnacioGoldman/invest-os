# Revenue Growth YoY

## What it means?

_Is the business growing right now?_

Compares the company's revenue in its latest reported quarter with the same quarter one year earlier. It shows how quickly the company's sales are growing or declining. For example, +15% means the company generated 15% more revenue than in the same quarter last year.

## How is calculated?

`(latest fiscal-quarter revenue / revenue in the same fiscal quarter last year − 1) × 100`.

## Tags

- **Strong:** revenue growth of 20% or more.
- **Solid:** revenue growth of at least 8% but less than 20%.
- **Flat:** revenue growth of 0% or more but less than 8%.
- **Weak:** negative revenue growth; sales declined compared with the same quarter last year.

Filters offer the same four tags. The **Flat** tag selects the 0–<8% band and is stored as **Flat growth** to distinguish it from older saved rules. Existing direction filters keep their meaning: **Growing** (>0%), **Flat** (=0%) and **Declining** (<0%). Opening a single direction rule in the editor presents its equivalent numeric comparison. **High Growth** and **Moderate Growth** are legacy aliases for Strong and Solid.
