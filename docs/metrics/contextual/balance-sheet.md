# Balance Sheet / Net Cash

## What it means?

_How do the company's cash holdings compare with its debt?_

Shows cash and cash equivalents, investments and debt separately. Net cash uses unrestricted cash equivalents less complete debt at the same reporting date and in the same currency.

## How is calculated?

`cash and cash equivalents − complete debt; investments excluded`.

Unrestricted cash means `CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue` or `CashAndCashEquivalents`. Exclude restricted-cash and cash-plus-investment aggregates from cash equivalents. Show short-term and long-term investments separately; neither establishes liquidity. Prefer short-term borrowings plus current/noncurrent long-term debt when all are available. Long-term debt alone, lease-only inputs or ambiguous aggregates do not establish complete debt. Retain component amounts, precise source tags, date, currency and scope even when net cash cannot be calculated.

## How to interpret it?

Cash restrictions, investment liquidity, leases and borrowing scope require review. Missing components are never zero. Partial debt cannot establish net cash; investments do not automatically count as liquid cash.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

Show cash, investments and debt components separately, with cash and debt history charts below. These charts describe reported component scope; they do not assume complete debt.
