# Financial Details

Contextual financial indicators are available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section. Each has a question, description, calculation, source, reporting period and neutral historical chart. They have no deterministic classification tags.

## Gross Margin

### What it means?

_How much revenue remains after the direct cost of sales?_

Measures quarterly gross profit as a percentage of revenue. It helps explain production and service costs before operating expenses.

### How is calculated?

`quarterly gross profit / same-quarter revenue × 100`.

### How to interpret it?

Business models, cost classifications and capital intensity differ. A margin level alone does not establish profitability or business quality.

## Price / Sales

### What it means?

_How much market value is assigned to each unit of sales?_

Compares current market capitalization with compatible trailing twelve-month revenue. Sales do not account for costs or reinvestment.

### How is calculated?

`current market capitalization / compatible TTM revenue`.

### How to interpret it?

Margins, growth, dilution and capital intensity affect interpretation. Require aligned reporting currency and a fresh market quote. Historical points use annual sales and year-end market capitalization.

## Free Cash Flow Yield

### What it means?

_How much free cash flow does the business generate relative to its market value?_

Expresses compatible trailing twelve-month free cash flow as a percentage of current market capitalization.

### How is calculated?

`compatible TTM free cash flow / current market capitalization × 100`.

### How to interpret it?

Working capital, capex timing and share-count estimates can distort the yield. Financial businesses are not comparable. Historical points use annual cash flow and year-end market capitalization.

## Data and display

Require positive revenue or market-cap denominators, compatible currencies and reporting periods, and fresh market quotes for valuation ratios. Financial-business FCF yield remains unavailable. Gross-margin history uses quarterly observations; Price/Sales and FCF Yield history uses annual financials and year-end market capitalization, explicitly labeled separately from the current TTM value.

Weighted-average diluted shares used for market capitalization, vendor adjustments, capex definitions and stale statements limit interpretation. Missing values are never zero. No qualitative ratings, filter matching, ranking contributions or automatic scores are assigned. Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification.
