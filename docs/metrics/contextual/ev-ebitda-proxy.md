# EV / EBITDA Proxy

## What it means?

_How does enterprise value compare with an estimate of operating cash earnings?_

Compares market capitalization plus debt less cash with operating income plus depreciation and amortization.

## How is calculated?

`(market capitalization + debt − cash) / (operating income + depreciation/amortization)`.

## How to interpret it?

Approximate EBITDA, incomplete debt, cash definitions, leases and mixed periods limit comparison. This proxy is especially unsuitable for financial businesses. Historical points use annual EBITDA and year-end enterprise value.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows Annual EV / EBITDA Proxy (year-end basis). Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
