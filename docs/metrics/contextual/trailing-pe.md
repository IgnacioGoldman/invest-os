# Trailing GAAP P/E

## What it means?

_How much market value is assigned to each unit of reported earnings?_

Compares current market capitalization with positive trailing twelve-month GAAP net income, using compatible listing currency.

## How is calculated?

`current market capitalization / positive TTM GAAP net income`.

## How to interpret it?

Exceptional or cyclically inflated earnings can make the multiple look low. Negative or tiny earnings defeat ordinary comparisons. Historical points use annual earnings and year-end market capitalization.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows Annual GAAP P/E (year-end basis). Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
