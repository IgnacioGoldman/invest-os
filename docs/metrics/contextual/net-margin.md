# Net Margin

## What it means?

_What share of revenue becomes reported net income?_

Measures quarterly GAAP net income as a percentage of quarterly revenue, including non-operating items and taxes.

## How is calculated?

`quarterly GAAP net income / same-quarter revenue × 100`.

## How to interpret it?

Investment gains, tax effects and other unusual items can overwhelm core profitability. Compare net income with operating income and reconcile exceptional items.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows Net Margin. Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
