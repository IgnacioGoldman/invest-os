# ROIC Proxy

## What it means?

_How does operating profit compare with the capital invested in the business?_

The saved proxy annualizes quarterly operating income against quarter-end debt plus equity less cash. It does not apply a NOPAT tax adjustment.

## How is calculated?

`quarterly operating income × 4 / (debt + equity − cash) × 100`.

## How to interpret it?

This proxy differs from standard ROIC. Debt scope, leases, cash treatment and near-zero or negative invested capital limit comparability.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows ROIC Proxy. Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
