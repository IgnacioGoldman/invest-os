# GAAP EPS Growth YoY

## What it means?

_How have reported earnings per share changed from a year ago?_

Compares GAAP diluted EPS in the latest quarter with the same fiscal quarter one year earlier, requiring positive comparable endpoints.

## How is calculated?

`(latest-quarter diluted EPS / same-quarter prior-year diluted EPS − 1) × 100`.

## How to interpret it?

Investment gains, tax benefits, buybacks and a small earnings base can dominate this rate. Review unusual items alongside operating performance.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows GAAP EPS Growth YoY. Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
