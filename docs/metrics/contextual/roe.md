# ROE (annualized quarterly)

## What it means?

_How does reported profit compare with shareholder equity?_

The saved quarterly calculation annualizes one quarter of net income against quarter-end equity. It differs from a return measured using annual earnings and average equity.

## How is calculated?

`quarterly net income × 4 / quarter-end shareholder equity × 100`.

## How to interpret it?

Unusual gains, buybacks and small or negative equity can inflate or invert returns. Compare earnings quality, average equity and sector-specific accounting.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows ROE (annualized quarterly). Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
