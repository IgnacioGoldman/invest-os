# EPS CAGR 3Y

## What it means?

_How quickly have reported earnings per share grown over three years?_

Expresses the change between comparable positive EPS endpoints three years apart as an annual compound growth rate.

## How is calculated?

`((latest EPS / EPS three years earlier) ** (1/3) − 1) × 100`.

## How to interpret it?

Exceptional endpoint gains, losses, cyclicality and share-count changes can dominate the result. Historical growth is not a forecast.

This is a contextual metric. It has no deterministic classification rules or tags and does not participate in saved-filter matching, rankings or automatic scores.

## Display and history

Available in the expandable **Contextual metrics** section of stock details. The card opens its question, description, calculation, reporting dates, source identifiers and explanatory notes. Values and charts use neutral styling. Missing values remain unavailable, never zero.

The chart shows EPS CAGR 3Y. Plot only available comparable historical observations. Annual year-end valuation points and quarterly observations must keep their stated basis; neither is silently relabeled as current TTM data.

Source identifiers preserve provenance, not independent verification. See the [metric index](../README.md).
